- How computers handle text files
- **Character-encoding**, you can try with extensions in the browser or in a text editor
- **String** = sequence of characters (other data types: integers, booleans, arrays)
- "**algorithms** automate tasks through formal description of descrete steps. Thinking algorithmically is partly about reducing large and interesting tasks to smaller steps."
- **Tokenisation**, in way that is understandable by a computer. Not about meaning, but e.g. "a sequence of characters with a space at either end". Tokenisation algorithms exist for most languages.
