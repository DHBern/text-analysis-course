# Homeworks

Upload the corpus of three novels as a zip archive:
- info on the [corpus](../README.md#corpus)
- [instructions](https://libvoyant.unm.edu/docs/#!/guide/corpuscreator) on how to upload multiple files (choose the zip archive)

Explore the themes of nature, war and characters' descriptions in the three novels. Choose some words and study their behaviours using at least two tools from Voyant Tools.

Emit an hypotheses starting from what you know about *The Great Gatsby* and map it back to the words you studied. Fill the protocol and upload it in ILIAS.
