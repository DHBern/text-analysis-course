# Homeworks


### Create a corpus
Create a zip archive with the three novels (see [corpus](../README.md#corpus)).

or

Split the txt file of *The Great Gatsby* in chapters, create as many txt files as there are chapters. Compress them all in a zip archive.

### Upload a corpus
Upload the zip archive:
- [instructions](https://libvoyant.unm.edu/docs/#!/guide/corpuscreator) on how to upload multiple files (choose the zip archive)

### Explore
Explore a theme (e.g. nature, light, war, money, characters' descriptions, or others) in your corpus. Choose some words and study their behaviours using at least two tools from Voyant Tools.

### Fill the protocol
Emit an hypotheses starting from what you know about *The Great Gatsby* and map it back to the words you studied in the corpus. Fill the protocol. Upload to ILIAS the protocol and the export of the visualisations in a zip archive called 'yoursurname_voyant.zip'.
